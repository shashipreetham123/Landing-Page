Our story

Ember & Oak began in 2019 as a single roaster in a garage and a stall at the Saturday market. Today we work directly with six farms, pay above fair-trade prices, and share the harvest details on every bag.

Our rule is simple: roast light enough to taste where the coffee grew, and dark enough to feel like coffee.
